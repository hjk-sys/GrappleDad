# Grapple Dad

A 2D platformer built in Godot 4 where you get around with a grappling hook: swing across gaps, pull yourself up to ledges, and dodge the enemies along the way.

## How to Play

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or ← / → |
| Jump | Space (press again in the air to double jump) |
| Grapple | Aim with the mouse, hold Left Click to hook, release to let go |
| Switch grapple mode | Shift |
| Pause | Esc |

## Mechanics

- **Swing Mode:** Hook onto a surface and swing around it. Hold left or right to build momentum, then let go to launch with a small speed boost.
- **Pull Mode:** Press Shift to switch modes. In Pull Mode, the hook reels you straight to where it landed.
- **Movement:** Double jump, wall slide and wall jump (hold toward the wall and press Space), and grab ledges automatically (press Space to climb up). Let go of Space early for a shorter jump.
- **Health:** You start with 5 hearts. Bats chase you through the air, ground enemies walk toward you, and hazards hurt on contact. Lose all your hearts and it's game over.
